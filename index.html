Tee Size Guide

Measurement Guide: These are garment measurements (laid flat), not body measurements. For the best fit, lay a T-shirt that fits you well on a flat surface and compare it with the chart.

A (Length): Measured from the highest point of the shoulder, next to the collar, straight down to the bottom hem.

B (Width): Measured straight across the chest, from one side seam to the other, about 1 inch (2.5 cm) below the armpit.

C (Sleeve Length): Measured from the center of the back neck seam to the end of the sleeve hem.

* Measurements may vary by up to ±1 inch (±2.5 cm) due to the manufacturing process and natural fabric relaxation.

Fit: Unisex, regular fit. If you are between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size.
